# MONARCH SOUND

## Premium International Music Label & Creative Audio Studio

MONARCH SOUND is a premium institutional web project created to represent an international music label focused on:

- Artist Development
- Music Production
- Sonic Identity
- Creative Culture
- Editorial Storytelling
- Curated Releases

The project was designed as a high-end creative company rather than a traditional music website.

It intentionally avoids:

- Festival aesthetics
- EDM visuals
- DJ portfolios
- Gaming visuals
- Cyberpunk styling
- Generic music templates

The visual direction combines inspiration from:

- Apple Music
- Monocle
- A24
- Bang & Olufsen
- COLORS Studios
- Erased Tapes
- Ninja Tune
- Saint Laurent

The goal is to create the perception of a real global creative company.
